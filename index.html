DOCUMENTARY

Mukto Dhara

Mukto Dhara is a documentary film by Kaushik Kabir Ghosh, produced for MUKTI, exploring the drinking water crisis in the Sundarbans and MUKTI's community-based water filtration initiative.

Script & Direction: Kaushik Kabir Ghosh
Cinematography: Surajit Das & Kaushik Kabir Ghosh
Editing: Dipak Mandal
Sound & Music: Biswajit Biswas
Colour: Sujoy Kumar Mishra
Client: MUKTI

About

FILMMAKER · DIRECTOR · SCREENWRITER

Stories rooted in people, places and reality.

Kaushik Kabir Ghosh is an independent filmmaker, director and screenwriter based in Kolkata, India. His work spans documentary films, corporate films, short-form visual storytelling and creative direction.

He is interested in stories rooted in people, places and the realities of everyday life, while developing narrative projects as a filmmaker, director and screenwriter.
